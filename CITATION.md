# Citing FPGAI Research

**Project:** FPGAI Research — Architecture-Aware FPGA Neural-Network Compilation and Source-Contract Semantics.

The repository is an academic research prototype. A final peer-reviewed paper citation, definitive coauthor list, release version, archival DOI and publication status **must not be inferred** from a development checkout.

Until an authorized paper or archival software release exists, describe any scientific result as originating from the **specific compiler source revision, study protocol and original evidence population** you actually used. Include the relevant FPGA target, numeric settings, Vitis/Vivado version and evidence qualification level when citing measured hardware results. Do not attribute synthesis estimates to routed or board-level observations.

Research on online Gym/RL policies is a **separate investigation** from the compiler's Model IR and policy-independent decision interface. Do not represent successful source certification as proof of trained-agent performance.
