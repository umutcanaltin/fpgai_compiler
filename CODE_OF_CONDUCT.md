# Project conduct and research collaboration

FPGAI Research aims to provide a technically rigorous, respectful environment for compiler, hardware and machine-learning researchers.

Contributors and maintainers should discuss designs and results on their merits; acknowledge other people's work; invite reproducible counterexamples; and avoid personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats or disclosure of another person's private information. Disagreement about scientific claims, numerical evidence or performance methodology is welcome when expressed constructively.

Respect confidentiality and intellectual-property boundaries. Do not publish another contributor's unpublished design, personal information, source-attested vendor output, private credentials, restricted model weights or proprietary institutional material without explicit authorization.

When reviewing work, distinguish experimental observations from hypotheses. A negative result, qualification failure or previously existing competing approach should be documented fairly rather than suppressed.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Contact the repository maintainer through a **private, previously established communication channel**. If an applicable platform private reporting mechanism exists, use it rather than revealing sensitive details in a public issue. Do not post confidential allegations or identifying information publicly.

Maintainers may request corrections, limit access or decline contributions in order to protect collaborators and the research record. No fixed response-time guarantee or formal dispute resolution process is asserted here.

This document governs collaboration expectations; it does **not** grant a software license or permission to publish the private repository.
