# Licensing direction: academic research only (not yet approved)

The repository owner has requested **research-only, non-commercial
source-available distribution** rather than a permissive or OSI open-source
license. The intended permitted users and activities are described in
[Who may use FPGAI?](WHO_CAN_USE_FPGAI.md).

**This is a policy preference, not a legally effective license.** The
private research repository currently has no approved `LICENSE` file.
Institutional/employer, third-party contributor and related intellectual
property reviews must finish before anyone can grant use or redistribution
rights for the exact released source files.

## Draft for review

The [Academic Research-Only Source License proposal](ACADEMIC_RESEARCH_LICENSE_PROPOSAL.md)
sets out requested eligibility, non-commercial use, attribution,
local modification, collaboration and commercial exclusion boundaries.
It is marked *PROPOSED / NOT EFFECTIVE*, has no fictitious named licensor
and does not grant rights until a real legal approval and final license
publication.

| Question | Proposed direction |
|---|---|
| Who may use it? | Researchers, students, educators, non-profit institutions and independent researchers doing non-commercial study |
| Can someone run HLS and build hardware for a paper? | Intended yes, only if vendor/third-party licenses and the final research license also allow it |
| Can a company use it for products or customer contracts? | No, negotiate separate commercial terms |
| Can someone publish a scientific analysis? | Intended yes, with truthful source citation and no restricted data disclosure |
| Can someone mirror/re-license the repository? | No general redistribution permission is proposed |
| Is this OSI open source? | **No**; non-commercial/research field-of-use restrictions are incompatible with the Open Source Definition |
| Is this usable under the draft today? | **No**; there is no authorized license grant yet |

The final text requires approval of the actual copyright holders,
institutional IP staff and appropriate legal counsel, particularly for
definitions of commercial research, generated RTL/bitstreams, derivative
works, access by collaborators, publication, warranty, jurisdiction and
termination.

**Do not add a fake `LICENSE` or claim final rights on behalf of the
unconfirmed owners.** Do not make the existing research repo public
without separately reviewing its full Git history. A clean-room public
source snapshot may be more suitable than exposing the original history.

See [Release checklist](OPEN_SOURCE_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) and
[Release preview](RELEASE_PREVIEW.md). This policy note does not
approve publication.
